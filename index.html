The Access Gap

M&A intelligence has always been
a luxury for the well-capitalized.

When a PE-backed consolidator targets an RIA, they arrive with a full research stack: curated deal pipelines, succession scoring models, proprietary contact databases, and a team of analysts running outreach. The seller often doesn't know it's happening until the LOI lands.

Independent advisors looking to grow through acquisition have none of that. They rely on word of mouth, broker referrals, and cold calls into a market they can barely see. By the time a quality firm surfaces, someone else has already been in that conversation for six months.

The same SEC filings that institutions pay six figures to license and analyze sit in a public database — structured, current, and rich with signals. The gap isn't data. It's access and interpretation.

🏦
PE firms run structured sourcing
Dedicated analysts, proprietary pipelines, and pre-built succession models. They know which firms are transitioning before the advisor does.
📊
ADV data is public but opaque
500+ pages of raw SEC filings per firm, updated quarterly. Revenue, client composition, succession signals — all there, none of it actionable without infrastructure.
⏱
Timing is everything in transitions
Succession conversations have a window. An advisor who surfaces the right firm 18 months early has a fundamentally different conversation than one who calls after a banker is engaged.
🎯
Match quality determines deal quality
Geographic fit, service overlap, compensation model, and cultural signals all predict integration success. Deals that start with a warm, intelligent match close faster and retain more clients.
