Beyond SEC Data

Half the market doesn't
appear in any
public database.

SEC Form ADV captures registered investment advisors. It doesn't capture the UBS team managing $340M who's been thinking about independence for three years. It doesn't capture the Ameriprise advisor who built a clean fee-only book inside a commission-first firm and wants out. It doesn't capture the Raymond James branch that's profitable, loyal, and quietly asking "what's next."

These advisors exist in the same market as registered RIAs. Established relationships, predictable AUM, less regulatory complexity — and no broker has them listed for sale. The information simply doesn't exist in any public source.

Meridian creates a private layer where both sides of that conversation can exist: SEC-registered firms in the database, wirehouse and BD advisors who profile themselves. The matching engine connects them privately. No banker. No public listing. No intermediary taking a cut.
